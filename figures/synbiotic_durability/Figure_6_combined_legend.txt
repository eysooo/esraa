Figure 6 | Durability of metabolic responses following synbiotic withdrawal. (A) Difference-in-differences estimates and 95% confidence intervals at 3 and 6 months for HOMA-IR, fasting insulin, LDL-C and HDL-C. HOMA-IR and fasting-insulin estimates are reported on the log scale, whereas lipid estimates are reported in mmol/L. (B) Model-estimated marginal means with 95% confidence intervals across baseline, 3 months and 6 months; the shaded interval denotes the 3-month post-intervention washout period. (C) Summary classification of response durability. P values are derived from the corresponding difference-in-differences contrasts, and q values were obtained using global Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate correction.
